The party starts at 2:00 and the trick or treating portion runs until 4:00! (You can come any time after 2:00) Costume judging starts at 4:00! 

The lights will be dimmed, 6 stations throughout the museum, and kiddos in their Halloween costumes can wander finding vintage Halloween decor on their scavenger hunt and be met by friendly, costumed treat-givers at the cars! When they finish their "road trip they will choose among different activities to do and have some refreshments while waiting for the judging to start at 4:00

 At 4:00 they will gather together and do a parade for the judges and costumes will be judged for some great prizes!  Costumes will be judged for Most Scary, Most Creative, and Most Funny, with prizes for the 3 winners! 

So plan to stay until 4:00! We may even have kiddos judge the museum adults that dressed up! 

Tell your friends! Bring a group, if you can! 

There will be non-food items available at the stations as well, for those with diet restrictions. Call 260-927-8022 if you have any questions!
