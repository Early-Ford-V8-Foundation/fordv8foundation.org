Help fill the Food Panty at the County Line Church of God in Auburn, IN for the holiday season by bringing your food donations to the Early Ford V8 Museum and we will give you a 1/2 price admission! We are collecting food donations the whole month of November.  Let us remember that there are many less fortunate than us that will be depending on our local food banks to help make their holiday a happy one. 

Many of our visitors are travelers who may not know about this endeavor, or perhaps a guest is local, forgot a canned good (or were not aware). NO WORRIES. Pay the regular admission price, but we will donate 1/2 that admission price to the food pantry. 

Thank you for your kindness in helping others! This is a win for everyone! Feel good about helping others while you enjoy this beautiful museum!
